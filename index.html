Est. 2026 Coverage: everywhere Status: coming soon
File 01 · The Mission

KindEarth starts with the people already doing the work: street feeders and everyday folks who see an animal being hurt and don't know where to turn. Our first app isn't for rescues, there are already good apps for that. It's for reporting cruelty the moment you see it, and getting you straight to the people who can actually act: AWBI members, legal teams, animal welfare experts, and verified volunteers nearby. You can even build your own group with people you trust, and grow it from there.

Report & Verify

Snap it, pin it, send it: photos, location, time. A real human checks every case before it goes anywhere.

The Right People

Verified reports go straight to AWBI members, legal teams, and animal welfare experts, whoever can actually do something about it.

Build Your Circle

Feeders and volunteers nearby can team up, form their own groups, and back each other up when it matters.

Learn & Protect

Know your rights as a reporter and the basics of animal welfare law, so you're never guessing what to do next.

How it
works

Four steps. Same deal, every time.

01

Spot

Something's off. An animal being hurt, starved, or mistreated right in front of you.

02

Document

A few photos, a pin on the map, a quick note. No confrontation, no heroics, just receipts.

03

File

It hits the log with a case number and an actual person reviewing it. Not a black hole.

04

Track

Watch it move: verified, routed, in progress, resolved. You're in the loop the whole way.

File 02 · What We're Watching

This is for reporting cruelty the moment you see it, not for rescue calls (there are already good apps for that). Here's the kind of thing that belongs in the log.

Animal cruelty or abuse Neglect or abandonment Poisoning or intentional harm Illegal animal trade Unsafe or unlicensed breeding Harassment of street animals Cruelty caught on camera Threats made to feeders
